# KPI: On-Time Delivery Rate
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of shipments delivered by the promised date.
**Formula:** On-Time Deliveries / Total Deliveries × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_shipments
**Used In Reports:** Supply Chain Dashboard (Power BI), Inventory Status Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** On-Time Delivery Rate is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
