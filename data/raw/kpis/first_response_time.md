# KPI: First Response Time
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average time to the first reply on a support ticket.
**Formula:** AVG(first_reply_time - created_time)
**Source Tables:** fact_tickets
**Used In Reports:** Customer Health Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Retention Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** First Response Time is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
