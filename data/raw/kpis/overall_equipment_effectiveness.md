# KPI: Overall Equipment Effectiveness
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Combined measure of availability, performance and quality of equipment.
**Formula:** Availability × Performance × Quality
**Source Tables:** fact_inventory
**Used In Reports:** Supply Chain Dashboard (Power BI), Inventory Status Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Overall Equipment Effectiveness is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
