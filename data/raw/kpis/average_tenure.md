# KPI: Average Tenure
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average years employees have worked at the company.
**Formula:** AVG(today - hire_date) in years
**Source Tables:** dim_employee
**Used In Reports:** Workforce Analytics Dashboard (Power BI), Headcount and Attrition Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Average Tenure is part of the HR KPI catalog.
