# KPI: Sales per Square Foot
**Domain:** Sales | **Owner:** Sales Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Retail revenue generated per square foot of store space.
**Formula:** Store Revenue / Store Area in sq ft
**Source Tables:** fact_sales, dim_date
**Used In Reports:** Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI), Daily Sales Summary (SSRS)
**Notes:** Sales per Square Foot is part of the Sales KPI catalog.
