# KPI: Employee Satisfaction Score
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average score from employee satisfaction surveys.
**Formula:** AVG(satisfaction_score)
**Source Tables:** fact_surveys, dim_employee
**Used In Reports:** Talent Acquisition Dashboard (Power BI), Recruitment Pipeline Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Employee Satisfaction Score is part of the HR KPI catalog.
