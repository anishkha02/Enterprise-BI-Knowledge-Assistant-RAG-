# KPI: High Performer Retention
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Retention of employees rated in the top performance band.
**Formula:** Top Performers Retained / Top Performers at Start × 100
**Source Tables:** dim_employee
**Used In Reports:** Workforce Analytics Dashboard (Power BI), Headcount and Attrition Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** High Performer Retention is part of the HR KPI catalog.
