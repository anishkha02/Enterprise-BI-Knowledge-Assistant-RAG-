# KPI: Operating Profit
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Profit from core operations before interest and tax.
**Formula:** Gross Profit - Operating Expenses
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, dim_account
**Used In Reports:** Financial Overview Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Profit and Loss (SSRS)
**Notes:** Operating Profit is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
