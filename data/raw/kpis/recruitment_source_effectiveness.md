# KPI: Recruitment Source Effectiveness
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Which recruiting channels produce the most hires.
**Formula:** Hires from Source / Total Hires × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_recruitment
**Used In Reports:** Talent Acquisition Dashboard (Power BI), Recruitment Pipeline Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Recruitment Source Effectiveness is part of the HR KPI catalog.
