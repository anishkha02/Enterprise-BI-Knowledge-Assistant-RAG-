# KPI: CLV to CAC Ratio
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Customer lifetime value compared with acquisition cost.
**Formula:** Customer Lifetime Value / Customer Acquisition Cost
**Source Tables:** dim_customer, fact_marketing
**Used In Reports:** Customer Health Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Retention Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** CLV to CAC Ratio is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
