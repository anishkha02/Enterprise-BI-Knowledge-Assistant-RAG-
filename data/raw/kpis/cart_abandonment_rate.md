# KPI: Cart Abandonment Rate
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of shopping carts that were not completed.
**Formula:** 1 - (Completed Orders / Carts Created) × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_orders, fact_marketing
**Used In Reports:** Support Operations Dashboard (Power BI), Support Ticket Summary (SSRS)
**Notes:** Cart Abandonment Rate is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
