# KPI: Inventory Turnover
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** How many times inventory is sold and replaced in a period.
**Formula:** COGS / Average Inventory
**Source Tables:** fact_inventory, dim_product
**Used In Reports:** Supply Chain Dashboard (Power BI), Inventory Status Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Inventory Turnover is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
