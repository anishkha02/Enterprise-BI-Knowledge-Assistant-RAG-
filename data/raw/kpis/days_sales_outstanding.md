# KPI: Days Sales Outstanding
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average days taken to collect payment after a sale.
**Formula:** Accounts Receivable / Revenue × Days in Period
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, fact_orders
**Used In Reports:** Financial Overview Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Profit and Loss (SSRS)
**Notes:** Days Sales Outstanding is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
