# KPI: Involuntary Attrition Rate
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of employees dismissed or laid off.
**Formula:** Terminations / Average Headcount × 100
**Source Tables:** dim_employee
**Used In Reports:** Talent Acquisition Dashboard (Power BI), Recruitment Pipeline Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Involuntary Attrition Rate is part of the HR KPI catalog.
