# KPI: Voluntary Attrition Rate
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of employees who resigned by their own choice.
**Formula:** Resignations / Average Headcount × 100
**Source Tables:** dim_employee
**Used In Reports:** Workforce Analytics Dashboard (Power BI), Headcount and Attrition Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Voluntary Attrition Rate is part of the HR KPI catalog.
