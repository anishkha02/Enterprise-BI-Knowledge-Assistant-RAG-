# KPI: Survey Response Rate
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of surveys sent that were completed.
**Formula:** Surveys Completed / Surveys Sent × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_surveys
**Used In Reports:** Customer Health Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Retention Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Survey Response Rate is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
