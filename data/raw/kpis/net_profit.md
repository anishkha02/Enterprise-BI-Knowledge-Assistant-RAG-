# KPI: Net Profit
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Profit remaining after all expenses, interest and taxes.
**Formula:** Revenue - All Expenses - Interest - Tax
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, dim_account
**Used In Reports:** Financial Overview Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Profit and Loss (SSRS)
**Notes:** Net Profit is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
