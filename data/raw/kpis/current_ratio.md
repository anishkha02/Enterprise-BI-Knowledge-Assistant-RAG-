# KPI: Current Ratio
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Ability to pay short-term obligations with current assets.
**Formula:** Current Assets / Current Liabilities
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, dim_account
**Used In Reports:** Budget Tracking Dashboard (Power BI), Budget vs Actual Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Current Ratio is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
