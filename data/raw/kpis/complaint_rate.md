# KPI: Complaint Rate
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Number of complaints per 1,000 orders.
**Formula:** Complaints / Orders × 1000
**Source Tables:** fact_tickets, fact_orders
**Used In Reports:** Support Operations Dashboard (Power BI), Support Ticket Summary (SSRS)
**Notes:** Complaint Rate is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
