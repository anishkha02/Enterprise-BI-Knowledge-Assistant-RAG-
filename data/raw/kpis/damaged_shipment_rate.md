# KPI: Damaged Shipment Rate
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of shipments that arrive damaged.
**Formula:** Damaged Shipments / Total Shipments × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_shipments
**Used In Reports:** Production Quality Dashboard (Power BI), Shipment Performance Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Damaged Shipment Rate is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
