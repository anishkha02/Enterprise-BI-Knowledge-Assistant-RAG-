# KPI: Internal Promotion Rate
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of roles filled by internal promotion.
**Formula:** Internal Promotions / Total Positions Filled × 100
**Source Tables:** dim_employee, fact_recruitment
**Used In Reports:** Talent Acquisition Dashboard (Power BI), Recruitment Pipeline Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Internal Promotion Rate is part of the HR KPI catalog.
