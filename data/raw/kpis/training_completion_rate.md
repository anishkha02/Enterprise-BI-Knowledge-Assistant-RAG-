# KPI: Training Completion Rate
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of assigned training courses completed.
**Formula:** Courses Completed / Courses Assigned × 100
**Source Tables:** dim_employee
**Used In Reports:** Workforce Analytics Dashboard (Power BI), Headcount and Attrition Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Training Completion Rate is part of the HR KPI catalog.
