# KPI: Inventory Accuracy
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** How closely system inventory matches physical counts.
**Formula:** Correct Counts / Total Counts × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_inventory
**Used In Reports:** Supply Chain Dashboard (Power BI), Inventory Status Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Inventory Accuracy is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
