# KPI: Revenue per Region
**Domain:** Sales | **Owner:** Sales Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Revenue split by customer sales region.
**Formula:** SUM(sales_amount) grouped by region
**Source Tables:** fact_sales, dim_customer
**Used In Reports:** Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI), Daily Sales Summary (SSRS)
**Notes:** Revenue per Region is part of the Sales KPI catalog.
