# KPI: Shipping Cost per Order
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average shipping cost per order.
**Formula:** Total Shipping Cost / Number of Orders
**Source Tables:** fact_shipments, fact_orders
**Used In Reports:** Production Quality Dashboard (Power BI), Shipment Performance Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Shipping Cost per Order is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
