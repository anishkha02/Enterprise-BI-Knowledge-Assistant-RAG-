# KPI: Order Picking Accuracy
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of picked orders with no errors.
**Formula:** Correct Picks / Total Picks × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_orders, fact_inventory
**Used In Reports:** Supply Chain Dashboard (Power BI), Inventory Status Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Order Picking Accuracy is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
