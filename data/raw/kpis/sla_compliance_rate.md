# KPI: SLA Compliance Rate
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of tickets resolved within the agreed SLA.
**Formula:** Tickets Within SLA / Total Tickets × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_tickets
**Used In Reports:** Support Operations Dashboard (Power BI), Support Ticket Summary (SSRS)
**Notes:** SLA Compliance Rate is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
