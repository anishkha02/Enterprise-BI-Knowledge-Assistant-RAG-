# KPI: Cost of Goods Sold
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Direct cost of producing the goods that were sold.
**Formula:** Opening Inventory + Purchases - Closing Inventory
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, fact_inventory
**Used In Reports:** Budget Tracking Dashboard (Power BI), Budget vs Actual Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Cost of Goods Sold is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
