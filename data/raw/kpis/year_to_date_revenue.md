# KPI: Year-to-Date Revenue
**Domain:** Sales | **Owner:** Sales Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Cumulative revenue from the start of the fiscal year to today.
**Formula:** SUM(sales_amount) from fiscal year start to today
**Source Tables:** fact_sales, dim_date
**Used In Reports:** Sales Pipeline Dashboard (Power BI), Regional Sales Detail (SSRS)
**Notes:** Year-to-Date Revenue is part of the Sales KPI catalog.
