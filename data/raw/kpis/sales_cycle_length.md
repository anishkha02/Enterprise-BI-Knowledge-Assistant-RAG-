# KPI: Sales Cycle Length
**Domain:** Sales | **Owner:** Sales Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average days from first lead contact to closed deal.
**Formula:** AVG(close_date - lead_created_date)
**Source Tables:** fact_orders, dim_date
**Used In Reports:** Sales Pipeline Dashboard (Power BI), Regional Sales Detail (SSRS)
**Notes:** Sales Cycle Length is part of the Sales KPI catalog.
