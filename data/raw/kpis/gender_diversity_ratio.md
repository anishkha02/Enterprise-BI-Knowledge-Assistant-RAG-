# KPI: Gender Diversity Ratio
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Share of employees by gender.
**Formula:** Employees of a Gender / Headcount × 100
**Source Tables:** dim_employee
**Used In Reports:** Workforce Analytics Dashboard (Power BI), Headcount and Attrition Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Gender Diversity Ratio is part of the HR KPI catalog.
