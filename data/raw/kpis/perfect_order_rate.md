# KPI: Perfect Order Rate
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of orders delivered on time, complete, undamaged and with correct documents.
**Formula:** Perfect Orders / Total Orders × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_orders, fact_shipments
**Used In Reports:** Production Quality Dashboard (Power BI), Shipment Performance Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Perfect Order Rate is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
