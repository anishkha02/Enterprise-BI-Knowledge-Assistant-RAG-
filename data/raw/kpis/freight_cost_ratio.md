# KPI: Freight Cost Ratio
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Freight cost as a share of net sales.
**Formula:** Freight Cost / Net Sales × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_shipments, fact_gl
**Used In Reports:** Supply Chain Dashboard (Power BI), Inventory Status Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Freight Cost Ratio is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
