# KPI: Net Margin
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Net profit as a percentage of revenue, after all expenses, interest and taxes.
**Formula:** Net Profit / Revenue × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, dim_account
**Used In Reports:** Budget Tracking Dashboard (Power BI), Budget vs Actual Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Net Margin is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
