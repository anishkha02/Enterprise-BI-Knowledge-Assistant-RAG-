# KPI: Ticket Backlog
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Number of support tickets that are still open.
**Formula:** COUNT(ticket_id) where status = Open
**Source Tables:** fact_tickets
**Used In Reports:** Support Operations Dashboard (Power BI), Support Ticket Summary (SSRS)
**Notes:** Ticket Backlog is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
