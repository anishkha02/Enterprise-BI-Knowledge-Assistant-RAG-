# KPI: Lead Conversion Rate
**Domain:** Sales | **Owner:** Sales Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of leads that become customers.
**Formula:** Converted Leads / Total Leads × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_orders, dim_customer
**Used In Reports:** Sales Pipeline Dashboard (Power BI), Regional Sales Detail (SSRS)
**Notes:** Lead Conversion Rate is part of the Sales KPI catalog.
