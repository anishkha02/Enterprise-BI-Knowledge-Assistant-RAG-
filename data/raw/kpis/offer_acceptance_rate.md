# KPI: Offer Acceptance Rate
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of job offers accepted by candidates.
**Formula:** Offers Accepted / Offers Made × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_recruitment
**Used In Reports:** Talent Acquisition Dashboard (Power BI), Recruitment Pipeline Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Offer Acceptance Rate is part of the HR KPI catalog.
