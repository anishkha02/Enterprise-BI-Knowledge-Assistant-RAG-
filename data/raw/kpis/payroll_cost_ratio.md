# KPI: Payroll Cost Ratio
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Payroll cost as a share of revenue.
**Formula:** Total Payroll Cost / Revenue × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_payroll, fact_gl
**Used In Reports:** Workforce Analytics Dashboard (Power BI), Headcount and Attrition Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Payroll Cost Ratio is part of the HR KPI catalog.
