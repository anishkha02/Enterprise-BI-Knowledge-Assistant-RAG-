# KPI: Retention Rate
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of employees who stay through the period.
**Formula:** (Headcount End - New Hires) / Headcount Start × 100
**Source Tables:** dim_employee
**Used In Reports:** Workforce Analytics Dashboard (Power BI), Headcount and Attrition Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Retention Rate is part of the HR KPI catalog.
