# KPI: Average Selling Price
**Domain:** Sales | **Owner:** Sales Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average price at which one unit was sold.
**Formula:** Total Revenue / Units Sold
**Source Tables:** fact_sales, dim_product
**Used In Reports:** Sales Pipeline Dashboard (Power BI), Regional Sales Detail (SSRS)
**Notes:** Average Selling Price is part of the Sales KPI catalog.
