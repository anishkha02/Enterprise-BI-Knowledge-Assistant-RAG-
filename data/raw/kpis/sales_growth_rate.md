# KPI: Sales Growth Rate
**Domain:** Sales | **Owner:** Sales Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage change in revenue compared with the previous period.
**Formula:** (Current Revenue - Prior Revenue) / Prior Revenue × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_sales, dim_date
**Used In Reports:** Sales Pipeline Dashboard (Power BI), Regional Sales Detail (SSRS)
**Notes:** Sales Growth Rate is part of the Sales KPI catalog.
