# KPI: Gross Profit
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Revenue minus the direct cost of goods sold.
**Formula:** Revenue - COGS
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, dim_account
**Used In Reports:** Financial Overview Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Profit and Loss (SSRS)
**Notes:** Gross Profit is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
