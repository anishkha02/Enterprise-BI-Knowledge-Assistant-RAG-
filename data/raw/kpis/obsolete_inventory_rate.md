# KPI: Obsolete Inventory Rate
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Share of inventory value that is obsolete.
**Formula:** Obsolete Inventory Value / Total Inventory Value × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_inventory, dim_product
**Used In Reports:** Production Quality Dashboard (Power BI), Shipment Performance Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Obsolete Inventory Rate is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
