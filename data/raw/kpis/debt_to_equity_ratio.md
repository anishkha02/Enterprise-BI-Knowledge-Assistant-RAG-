# KPI: Debt to Equity Ratio
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Amount of debt used relative to shareholder equity.
**Formula:** Total Debt / Shareholder Equity
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, dim_account
**Used In Reports:** Budget Tracking Dashboard (Power BI), Budget vs Actual Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Debt to Equity Ratio is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
