# KPI: Average Revenue per Customer
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average revenue generated per active customer.
**Formula:** Total Revenue / Active Customers
**Source Tables:** dim_customer, fact_orders
**Used In Reports:** Customer Health Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Retention Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Average Revenue per Customer is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
