# KPI: Time to Hire
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average days from job posting to offer acceptance.
**Formula:** AVG(offer_accepted_date - posting_date)
**Source Tables:** fact_recruitment
**Used In Reports:** Talent Acquisition Dashboard (Power BI), Recruitment Pipeline Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Time to Hire is part of the HR KPI catalog.
