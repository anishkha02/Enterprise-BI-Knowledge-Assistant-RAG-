# KPI: Carrying Cost of Inventory
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Total cost of holding inventory.
**Formula:** Storage + Insurance + Capital + Obsolescence Costs
**Source Tables:** fact_inventory, fact_gl
**Used In Reports:** Production Quality Dashboard (Power BI), Shipment Performance Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Carrying Cost of Inventory is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
