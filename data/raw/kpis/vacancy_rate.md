# KPI: Vacancy Rate
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Open positions as a share of total positions.
**Formula:** Open Positions / Total Positions × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_recruitment, dim_department
**Used In Reports:** Talent Acquisition Dashboard (Power BI), Recruitment Pipeline Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Vacancy Rate is part of the HR KPI catalog.
