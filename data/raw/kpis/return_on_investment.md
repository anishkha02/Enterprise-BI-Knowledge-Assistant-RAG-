# KPI: Return on Investment
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Gain from an investment relative to its cost.
**Formula:** (Gain - Cost) / Cost × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_gl
**Used In Reports:** Financial Overview Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Profit and Loss (SSRS)
**Notes:** Return on Investment is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
