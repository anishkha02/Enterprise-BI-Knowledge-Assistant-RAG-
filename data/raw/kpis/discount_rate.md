# KPI: Discount Rate
**Domain:** Sales | **Owner:** Sales Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Share of list price given away as discounts.
**Formula:** Total Discount / Gross Sales × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_sales
**Used In Reports:** Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI), Daily Sales Summary (SSRS)
**Notes:** Discount Rate is part of the Sales KPI catalog.
