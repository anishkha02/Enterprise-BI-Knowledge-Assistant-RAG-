# KPI: Overtime Rate
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Overtime hours as a share of regular hours.
**Formula:** Overtime Hours / Regular Hours × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_payroll
**Used In Reports:** Talent Acquisition Dashboard (Power BI), Recruitment Pipeline Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Overtime Rate is part of the HR KPI catalog.
