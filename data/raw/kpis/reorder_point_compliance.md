# KPI: Reorder Point Compliance
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of reorders placed at the right reorder point.
**Formula:** Reorders at Correct Point / Total Reorders × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_inventory, dim_product
**Used In Reports:** Supply Chain Dashboard (Power BI), Inventory Status Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Reorder Point Compliance is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
