# KPI: Customer Effort Score
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** How easy it was for customers to get an issue resolved.
**Formula:** AVG(effort_rating)
**Source Tables:** fact_surveys, fact_tickets
**Used In Reports:** Support Operations Dashboard (Power BI), Support Ticket Summary (SSRS)
**Notes:** Customer Effort Score is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
