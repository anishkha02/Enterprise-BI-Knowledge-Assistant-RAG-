# KPI: Accounts Receivable Turnover
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** How many times receivables are collected in a period.
**Formula:** Net Credit Sales / Average Accounts Receivable
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, fact_orders
**Used In Reports:** Financial Overview Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Profit and Loss (SSRS)
**Notes:** Accounts Receivable Turnover is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
