# KPI: Warehouse Utilization
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of warehouse capacity in use.
**Formula:** Used Storage Space / Total Storage Space × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_inventory
**Used In Reports:** Supply Chain Dashboard (Power BI), Inventory Status Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Warehouse Utilization is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
