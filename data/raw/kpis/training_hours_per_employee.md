# KPI: Training Hours per Employee
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average training hours completed per employee.
**Formula:** Total Training Hours / Headcount
**Source Tables:** dim_employee, dim_department
**Used In Reports:** Talent Acquisition Dashboard (Power BI), Recruitment Pipeline Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Training Hours per Employee is part of the HR KPI catalog.
