# KPI: Order Fulfillment Rate
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of orders shipped complete from stock.
**Formula:** Orders Fulfilled Complete / Total Orders × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_orders, fact_shipments
**Used In Reports:** Production Quality Dashboard (Power BI), Shipment Performance Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Order Fulfillment Rate is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
