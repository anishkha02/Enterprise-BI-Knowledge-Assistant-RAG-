# KPI: Repeat Purchase Rate
**Domain:** Sales | **Owner:** Sales Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of orders placed by customers who had bought before.
**Formula:** Orders from Returning Customers / Total Orders × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_orders, dim_customer
**Used In Reports:** Sales Pipeline Dashboard (Power BI), Regional Sales Detail (SSRS)
**Notes:** Repeat Purchase Rate is part of the Sales KPI catalog.
