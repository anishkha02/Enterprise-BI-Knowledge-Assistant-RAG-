# KPI: First Contact Resolution Rate
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of tickets solved in the first interaction.
**Formula:** Tickets Solved at First Contact / Total Tickets × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_tickets
**Used In Reports:** Customer Health Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Retention Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** First Contact Resolution Rate is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
