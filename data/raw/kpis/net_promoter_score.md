# KPI: Net Promoter Score
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Likelihood that customers recommend the company.
**Formula:** % Promoters - % Detractors
**Source Tables:** fact_surveys, dim_customer
**Used In Reports:** Support Operations Dashboard (Power BI), Support Ticket Summary (SSRS)
**Notes:** Net Promoter Score is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
