# KPI: EBITDA
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization.
**Formula:** Operating Profit + Depreciation + Amortization
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, dim_account
**Used In Reports:** Financial Overview Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Profit and Loss (SSRS)
**Notes:** EBITDA is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
