# KPI: Revenue per Channel
**Domain:** Sales | **Owner:** Sales Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Revenue split by sales channel such as online, retail or partner.
**Formula:** SUM(sales_amount) grouped by channel
**Source Tables:** fact_sales
**Used In Reports:** Sales Pipeline Dashboard (Power BI), Regional Sales Detail (SSRS)
**Notes:** Revenue per Channel is part of the Sales KPI catalog.
