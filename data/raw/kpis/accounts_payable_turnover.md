# KPI: Accounts Payable Turnover
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** How many times payables are paid in a period.
**Formula:** Purchases / Average Accounts Payable
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, dim_account
**Used In Reports:** Budget Tracking Dashboard (Power BI), Budget vs Actual Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Accounts Payable Turnover is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
