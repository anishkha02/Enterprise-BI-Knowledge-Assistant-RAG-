# KPI: Capital Expenditure Ratio
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Capital expenditure as a share of revenue.
**Formula:** Capital Expenditure / Revenue × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, dim_account
**Used In Reports:** Financial Overview Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Profit and Loss (SSRS)
**Notes:** Capital Expenditure Ratio is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
