# KPI: Cost per Unit Produced
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average cost to make one unit.
**Formula:** Total Production Cost / Units Produced
**Source Tables:** fact_inventory, fact_gl
**Used In Reports:** Supply Chain Dashboard (Power BI), Inventory Status Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Cost per Unit Produced is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
