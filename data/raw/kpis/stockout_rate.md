# KPI: Stockout Rate
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of demand lost because items were out of stock.
**Formula:** Stockout Events / Total Demand Events × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_inventory, dim_product
**Used In Reports:** Supply Chain Dashboard (Power BI), Inventory Status Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Stockout Rate is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
