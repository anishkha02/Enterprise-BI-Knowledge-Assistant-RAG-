# KPI: Sales Target Attainment
**Domain:** Sales | **Owner:** Sales Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** How much of the sales target was achieved.
**Formula:** Actual Revenue / Target Revenue × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_sales, dim_date
**Used In Reports:** Sales Pipeline Dashboard (Power BI), Regional Sales Detail (SSRS)
**Notes:** Sales Target Attainment is part of the Sales KPI catalog.
