# KPI: Top Product Revenue Share
**Domain:** Sales | **Owner:** Sales Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Share of total revenue coming from the top 10 products.
**Formula:** Revenue of Top 10 Products / Total Revenue × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_sales, dim_product
**Used In Reports:** Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI), Daily Sales Summary (SSRS)
**Notes:** Top Product Revenue Share is part of the Sales KPI catalog.
