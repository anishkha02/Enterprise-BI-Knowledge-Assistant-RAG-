# KPI: Reopen Rate
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of resolved tickets that were reopened.
**Formula:** Reopened Tickets / Resolved Tickets × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_tickets
**Used In Reports:** Customer Health Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Retention Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Reopen Rate is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
