# KPI: Customer Acquisition Cost
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average marketing and sales cost to win one new customer.
**Formula:** Total Acquisition Spend / New Customers
**Source Tables:** fact_marketing, dim_customer
**Used In Reports:** Support Operations Dashboard (Power BI), Support Ticket Summary (SSRS)
**Notes:** Customer Acquisition Cost is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
