# KPI: Backorder Rate
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of orders that could not be filled immediately.
**Formula:** Backordered Orders / Total Orders × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_orders, fact_inventory
**Used In Reports:** Production Quality Dashboard (Power BI), Shipment Performance Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Backorder Rate is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
