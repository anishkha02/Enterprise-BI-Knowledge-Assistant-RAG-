# KPI: Days Payable Outstanding
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average days taken to pay suppliers.
**Formula:** Accounts Payable / COGS × Days in Period
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, dim_account
**Used In Reports:** Budget Tracking Dashboard (Power BI), Budget vs Actual Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Days Payable Outstanding is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
