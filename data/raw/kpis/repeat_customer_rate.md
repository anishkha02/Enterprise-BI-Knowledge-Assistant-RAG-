# KPI: Repeat Customer Rate
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of all customers who have ordered more than once.
**Formula:** Customers with 2+ Orders / Total Customers × 100
**Source Tables:** dim_customer, fact_orders
**Used In Reports:** Customer Health Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Retention Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Repeat Customer Rate is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
