# KPI: Budget Variance
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Difference between actual spending and budgeted spending.
**Formula:** Actual Expense - Budgeted Expense
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, dim_account
**Used In Reports:** Financial Overview Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Profit and Loss (SSRS)
**Notes:** Budget Variance is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
