# KPI: Cost per Hire
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average spend to recruit one employee.
**Formula:** Total Recruiting Cost / Number of Hires
**Source Tables:** fact_recruitment
**Used In Reports:** Workforce Analytics Dashboard (Power BI), Headcount and Attrition Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Cost per Hire is part of the HR KPI catalog.
