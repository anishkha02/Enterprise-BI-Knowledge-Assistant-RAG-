# KPI: Customer Health Score
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Composite score of usage, satisfaction and support history.
**Formula:** Weighted Sum of Usage, CSAT and Ticket Scores
**Source Tables:** dim_customer, fact_surveys, fact_tickets
**Used In Reports:** Customer Health Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Retention Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Customer Health Score is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
