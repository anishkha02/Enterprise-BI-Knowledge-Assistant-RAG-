# KPI: Return on Equity
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Profit generated per unit of shareholder equity.
**Formula:** Net Profit / Shareholder Equity × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, dim_account
**Used In Reports:** Budget Tracking Dashboard (Power BI), Budget vs Actual Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Return on Equity is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
