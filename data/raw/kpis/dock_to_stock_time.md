# KPI: Dock to Stock Time
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average hours from goods arriving at the dock to being available in stock.
**Formula:** AVG(stock_time - dock_time)
**Source Tables:** fact_inventory
**Used In Reports:** Production Quality Dashboard (Power BI), Shipment Performance Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Dock to Stock Time is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
