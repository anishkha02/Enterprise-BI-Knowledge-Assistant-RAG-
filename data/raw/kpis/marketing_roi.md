# KPI: Marketing ROI
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Return generated per unit spent on marketing.
**Formula:** (Campaign Revenue - Campaign Cost) / Campaign Cost × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_marketing, fact_gl
**Used In Reports:** Customer Health Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Retention Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Marketing ROI is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
