# KPI: Gross Margin
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Gross profit as a percentage of revenue, before operating expenses are deducted.
**Formula:** Gross Profit / Revenue × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, dim_account
**Used In Reports:** Budget Tracking Dashboard (Power BI), Budget vs Actual Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Gross Margin is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
