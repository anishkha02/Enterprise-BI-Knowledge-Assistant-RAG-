# KPI: Employee Engagement Index
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Composite score of engagement survey questions.
**Formula:** AVG(engagement question scores)
**Source Tables:** fact_surveys
**Used In Reports:** Workforce Analytics Dashboard (Power BI), Headcount and Attrition Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Employee Engagement Index is part of the HR KPI catalog.
