# KPI: Average Delivery Time
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average days from order to delivery.
**Formula:** AVG(delivery_date - order_date)
**Source Tables:** fact_shipments, fact_orders
**Used In Reports:** Supply Chain Dashboard (Power BI), Inventory Status Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Average Delivery Time is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
