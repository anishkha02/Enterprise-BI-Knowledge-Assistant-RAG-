# KPI: Forecast Accuracy
**Domain:** Sales | **Owner:** Sales Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** How close the sales forecast was to actual revenue.
**Formula:** (1 - ABS(Actual - Forecast) / Actual) × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_sales, dim_date
**Used In Reports:** Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI), Daily Sales Summary (SSRS)
**Notes:** Forecast Accuracy is part of the Sales KPI catalog.
