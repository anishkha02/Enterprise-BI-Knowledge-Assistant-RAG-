# KPI: Scrap Rate
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of materials wasted during production.
**Formula:** Scrap Quantity / Total Material Used × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_inventory, dim_product
**Used In Reports:** Production Quality Dashboard (Power BI), Shipment Performance Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Scrap Rate is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
