# KPI: Upsell Rate
**Domain:** Sales | **Owner:** Sales Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of orders where the customer chose a higher-tier product.
**Formula:** Upsold Orders / Total Orders × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_orders, dim_product
**Used In Reports:** Sales Pipeline Dashboard (Power BI), Regional Sales Detail (SSRS)
**Notes:** Upsell Rate is part of the Sales KPI catalog.
