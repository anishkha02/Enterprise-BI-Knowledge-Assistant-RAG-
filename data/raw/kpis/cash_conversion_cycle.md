# KPI: Cash Conversion Cycle
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Days needed to convert inventory investment into cash.
**Formula:** DIO + DSO - DPO
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, fact_inventory
**Used In Reports:** Financial Overview Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Profit and Loss (SSRS)
**Notes:** Cash Conversion Cycle is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
