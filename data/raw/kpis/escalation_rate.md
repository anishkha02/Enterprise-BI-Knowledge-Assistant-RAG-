# KPI: Escalation Rate
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of tickets escalated to a higher support tier.
**Formula:** Escalated Tickets / Total Tickets × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_tickets
**Used In Reports:** Support Operations Dashboard (Power BI), Support Ticket Summary (SSRS)
**Notes:** Escalation Rate is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
