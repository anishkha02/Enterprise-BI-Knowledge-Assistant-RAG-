# KPI: Quick Ratio
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Liquidity measure that excludes inventory.
**Formula:** (Current Assets - Inventory) / Current Liabilities
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, dim_account
**Used In Reports:** Financial Overview Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Profit and Loss (SSRS)
**Notes:** Quick Ratio is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
