# KPI: Customer Churn Rate
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of customers who stopped purchasing during a period. Customers inactive for 90+ days are considered churned.
**Formula:** (Customers Lost in Period / Customers at Start of Period) × 100
**Source Tables:** dim_customer, fact_orders
**Used In Reports:** Customer Health Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Retention Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Customer Churn Rate is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
