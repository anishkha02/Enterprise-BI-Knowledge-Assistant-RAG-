# KPI: Employee Net Promoter Score
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Likelihood that employees recommend the company as a workplace.
**Formula:** % Promoters - % Detractors
**Source Tables:** fact_surveys, dim_employee
**Used In Reports:** Workforce Analytics Dashboard (Power BI), Headcount and Attrition Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Employee Net Promoter Score is part of the HR KPI catalog.
