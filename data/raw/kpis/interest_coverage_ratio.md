# KPI: Interest Coverage Ratio
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Ability to pay interest from operating profit.
**Formula:** Operating Profit / Interest Expense
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, dim_account
**Used In Reports:** Financial Overview Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Profit and Loss (SSRS)
**Notes:** Interest Coverage Ratio is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
