# KPI: Leave Utilization Rate
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Share of entitled leave days that employees have taken.
**Formula:** Leave Days Taken / Leave Days Entitled × 100
**Source Tables:** dim_employee, fact_payroll
**Used In Reports:** Talent Acquisition Dashboard (Power BI), Recruitment Pipeline Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Leave Utilization Rate is part of the HR KPI catalog.
