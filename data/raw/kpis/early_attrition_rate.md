# KPI: Early Attrition Rate
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of new hires who leave within the first 90 days.
**Formula:** Leavers within 90 Days / New Hires × 100
**Source Tables:** dim_employee, fact_recruitment
**Used In Reports:** Talent Acquisition Dashboard (Power BI), Recruitment Pipeline Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Early Attrition Rate is part of the HR KPI catalog.
