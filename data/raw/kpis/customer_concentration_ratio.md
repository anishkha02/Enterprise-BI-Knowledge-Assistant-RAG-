# KPI: Customer Concentration Ratio
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Share of revenue coming from the top 10 customers.
**Formula:** Top 10 Customer Revenue / Total Revenue × 100
**Source Tables:** dim_customer, fact_orders
**Used In Reports:** Support Operations Dashboard (Power BI), Support Ticket Summary (SSRS)
**Notes:** Customer Concentration Ratio is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
