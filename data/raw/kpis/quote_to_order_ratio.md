# KPI: Quote-to-Order Ratio
**Domain:** Sales | **Owner:** Sales Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Share of sales quotes that turn into orders.
**Formula:** Orders Created / Quotes Issued × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_orders
**Used In Reports:** Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI), Daily Sales Summary (SSRS)
**Notes:** Quote-to-Order Ratio is part of the Sales KPI catalog.
