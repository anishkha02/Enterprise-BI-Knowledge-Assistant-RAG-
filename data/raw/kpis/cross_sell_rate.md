# KPI: Cross-Sell Rate
**Domain:** Sales | **Owner:** Sales Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of orders containing products from more than one category.
**Formula:** Multi-Category Orders / Total Orders × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_orders, dim_product
**Used In Reports:** Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI), Daily Sales Summary (SSRS)
**Notes:** Cross-Sell Rate is part of the Sales KPI catalog.
