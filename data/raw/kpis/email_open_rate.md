# KPI: Email Open Rate
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of marketing emails that were opened.
**Formula:** Emails Opened / Emails Delivered × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_marketing
**Used In Reports:** Customer Health Dashboard (Power BI), Monthly Retention Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Email Open Rate is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
