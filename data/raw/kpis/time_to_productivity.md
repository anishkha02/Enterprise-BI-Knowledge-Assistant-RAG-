# KPI: Time to Productivity
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average days for a new hire to reach full productivity.
**Formula:** AVG(productive_date - hire_date)
**Source Tables:** dim_employee
**Used In Reports:** Workforce Analytics Dashboard (Power BI), Headcount and Attrition Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Time to Productivity is part of the HR KPI catalog.
