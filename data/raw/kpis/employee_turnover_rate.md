# KPI: Employee Turnover Rate
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of employees who left during the period.
**Formula:** Employees Who Left / Average Headcount × 100
**Source Tables:** dim_employee, dim_department
**Used In Reports:** Talent Acquisition Dashboard (Power BI), Recruitment Pipeline Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Employee Turnover Rate is part of the HR KPI catalog.
