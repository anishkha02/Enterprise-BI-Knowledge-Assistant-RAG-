# KPI: Reactivated Customers
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Previously churned customers who purchased again.
**Formula:** COUNT(churned customers with a new order)
**Source Tables:** dim_customer, fact_orders
**Used In Reports:** Support Operations Dashboard (Power BI), Support Ticket Summary (SSRS)
**Notes:** Reactivated Customers is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
