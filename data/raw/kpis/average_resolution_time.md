# KPI: Average Resolution Time
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average time to fully resolve support tickets.
**Formula:** AVG(resolved_time - created_time)
**Source Tables:** fact_tickets
**Used In Reports:** Support Operations Dashboard (Power BI), Support Ticket Summary (SSRS)
**Notes:** Average Resolution Time is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
