# KPI: Days Inventory Outstanding
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average days inventory is held before it is sold.
**Formula:** Average Inventory / COGS × Days in Period
**Source Tables:** fact_inventory
**Used In Reports:** Production Quality Dashboard (Power BI), Shipment Performance Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Days Inventory Outstanding is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
