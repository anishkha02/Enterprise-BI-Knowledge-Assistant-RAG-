# KPI: Burn Rate
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Net cash spent per month.
**Formula:** (Opening Cash - Closing Cash) / Months
**Source Tables:** fact_gl
**Used In Reports:** Budget Tracking Dashboard (Power BI), Budget vs Actual Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Burn Rate is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
