# KPI: Compensation Ratio
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average salary relative to the market midpoint.
**Formula:** Average Salary / Market Midpoint Salary
**Source Tables:** fact_payroll, dim_employee
**Used In Reports:** Workforce Analytics Dashboard (Power BI), Headcount and Attrition Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Compensation Ratio is part of the HR KPI catalog.
