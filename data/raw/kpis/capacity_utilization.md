# KPI: Capacity Utilization
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Share of production capacity actually used.
**Formula:** Actual Output / Maximum Possible Output × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_inventory
**Used In Reports:** Supply Chain Dashboard (Power BI), Inventory Status Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Capacity Utilization is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
