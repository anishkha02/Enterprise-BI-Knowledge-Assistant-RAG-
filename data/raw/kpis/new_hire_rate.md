# KPI: New Hire Rate
**Domain:** HR | **Owner:** HR Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** New employees as a share of average headcount.
**Formula:** New Hires / Average Headcount × 100
**Source Tables:** dim_employee, fact_recruitment
**Used In Reports:** Workforce Analytics Dashboard (Power BI), Headcount and Attrition Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** New Hire Rate is part of the HR KPI catalog.
