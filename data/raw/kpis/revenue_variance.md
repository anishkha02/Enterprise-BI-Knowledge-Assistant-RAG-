# KPI: Revenue Variance
**Domain:** Finance | **Owner:** Finance Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Difference between actual and budgeted revenue.
**Formula:** Actual Revenue - Budgeted Revenue
**Source Tables:** fact_gl, dim_account
**Used In Reports:** Budget Tracking Dashboard (Power BI), Budget vs Actual Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Revenue Variance is part of the Finance KPI catalog.
