# KPI: Revenue per Product Category
**Domain:** Sales | **Owner:** Sales Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Revenue split by product category.
**Formula:** SUM(sales_amount) grouped by category
**Source Tables:** fact_sales, dim_product
**Used In Reports:** Sales Pipeline Dashboard (Power BI), Regional Sales Detail (SSRS)
**Notes:** Revenue per Product Category is part of the Sales KPI catalog.
