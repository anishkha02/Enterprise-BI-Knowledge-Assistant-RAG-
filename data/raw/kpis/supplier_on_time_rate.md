# KPI: Supplier On-Time Rate
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of supplier deliveries received on time.
**Formula:** On-Time Supplier Deliveries / Total Supplier Deliveries × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_inventory
**Used In Reports:** Supply Chain Dashboard (Power BI), Inventory Status Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Supplier On-Time Rate is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
