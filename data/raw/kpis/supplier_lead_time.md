# KPI: Supplier Lead Time
**Domain:** Operations | **Owner:** Operations Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Average days from purchase order to goods receipt.
**Formula:** AVG(receipt_date - po_date)
**Source Tables:** fact_inventory
**Used In Reports:** Production Quality Dashboard (Power BI), Shipment Performance Report (SSRS)
**Notes:** Supplier Lead Time is part of the Operations KPI catalog.
