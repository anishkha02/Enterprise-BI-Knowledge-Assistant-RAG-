# KPI: Campaign Conversion Rate
**Domain:** Customer | **Owner:** Customer Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Percentage of campaign recipients who purchased.
**Formula:** Purchasers / Recipients × 100
**Source Tables:** fact_marketing, fact_orders
**Used In Reports:** Support Operations Dashboard (Power BI), Support Ticket Summary (SSRS)
**Notes:** Campaign Conversion Rate is part of the Customer KPI catalog.
