# KPI: New Customer Revenue
**Domain:** Sales | **Owner:** Sales Analytics | **Refresh:** Monthly
**Definition:** Revenue from customers making their first purchase.
**Formula:** SUM(sales_amount) where customer is new
**Source Tables:** fact_sales, dim_customer
**Used In Reports:** Sales Pipeline Dashboard (Power BI), Regional Sales Detail (SSRS)
**Notes:** New Customer Revenue is part of the Sales KPI catalog.
