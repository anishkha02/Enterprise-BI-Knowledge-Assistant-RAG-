# Power BI Dashboard: Sales Performance Dashboard
**Domain:** Sales
**Pages:** Overview, Regional Analysis, Product Analysis
**Key Measure:** Total Revenue
**DAX:** Total Revenue = SUM(fact_sales[sales_amount])
**Filters:** Date range slicer, Region slicer
**Scheduled Refresh:** Every 4 hours
**KPIs Included:** Total Revenue, Units Sold, Revenue per Region, Top Product Revenue Share, Win Rate, Pipeline Coverage, Average Deal Size, Discount Rate, Net Sales, Cross-Sell Rate, Quote-to-Order Ratio, Existing Customer Revenue, Online Sales Share, Sales per Square Foot, Forecast Accuracy
