# Power BI Dashboard: Workforce Analytics Dashboard
**Domain:** HR
**Pages:** Headcount, Attrition, Diversity
**Key Measure:** Turnover Rate
**DAX:** Turnover Rate = DIVIDE([Leavers], [Average Headcount])
**Filters:** Date range slicer, Region slicer
**Scheduled Refresh:** Daily at 4:00 AM
**KPIs Included:** Headcount, Voluntary Attrition Rate, New Hire Rate, Cost per Hire, Retention Rate, Average Tenure, Employee Net Promoter Score, Training Completion Rate, Payroll Cost Ratio, Gender Diversity Ratio, Span of Control, Time to Productivity, High Performer Retention, Compensation Ratio, Employee Engagement Index
