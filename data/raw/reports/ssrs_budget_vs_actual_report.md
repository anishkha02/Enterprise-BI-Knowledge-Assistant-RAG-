# SSRS Report: Budget vs Actual Report
**Domain:** Finance
**Purpose:** Paginated Finance report for operational review.
**Parameters:** Fiscal Year, Department
**Datasets:** dim_account, fact_gl, fact_inventory
**Refresh Schedule:** Monthly on the 5th
**KPIs Included:** Gross Margin, Net Margin, Operating Margin, EBITDA Margin, Cost of Goods Sold, Revenue Variance, Return on Equity, Current Ratio, Debt to Equity Ratio, Days Payable Outstanding, Operating Cash Flow, Working Capital, Accounts Payable Turnover, Burn Rate
