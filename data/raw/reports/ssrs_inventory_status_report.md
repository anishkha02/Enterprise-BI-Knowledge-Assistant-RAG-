# SSRS Report: Inventory Status Report
**Domain:** Operations
**Purpose:** Paginated Operations report for operational review.
**Parameters:** Warehouse, As-Of Date
**Datasets:** dim_product, fact_gl, fact_inventory, fact_orders, fact_shipments
**Refresh Schedule:** Daily at 5:00 AM
**KPIs Included:** Inventory Turnover, Stockout Rate, On-Time Delivery Rate, Average Delivery Time, Order Cycle Time, Inventory Accuracy, Warehouse Utilization, Supplier On-Time Rate, First Pass Yield, Capacity Utilization, Overall Equipment Effectiveness, Cost per Unit Produced, Freight Cost Ratio, Order Picking Accuracy, Reorder Point Compliance
