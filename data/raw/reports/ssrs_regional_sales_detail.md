# SSRS Report: Regional Sales Detail
**Domain:** Sales
**Purpose:** Paginated Sales report for operational review.
**Parameters:** Fiscal Year, Region, Product Category
**Datasets:** dim_customer, dim_date, dim_employee, dim_product, fact_orders, fact_sales
**Refresh Schedule:** Weekly on Monday at 7:00 AM
**KPIs Included:** Average Order Value, Sales Growth Rate, Revenue per Product Category, Sales Target Attainment, Sales Cycle Length, Lead Conversion Rate, Revenue per Salesperson, Return Rate, Repeat Purchase Rate, Upsell Rate, New Customer Revenue, Revenue per Channel, Average Selling Price, Backlog Value, Year-to-Date Revenue
