# Power BI Dashboard: Talent Acquisition Dashboard
**Domain:** HR
**Pages:** Hiring Funnel, Time to Hire
**Key Measure:** Offer Acceptance Rate
**DAX:** Offer Acceptance Rate = DIVIDE([Offers Accepted], [Offers Made])
**Filters:** Date range slicer, Region slicer
**Scheduled Refresh:** Daily at 4:30 AM
**KPIs Included:** Employee Turnover Rate, Involuntary Attrition Rate, Time to Hire, Offer Acceptance Rate, Absenteeism Rate, Employee Satisfaction Score, Training Hours per Employee, Revenue per Employee, Overtime Rate, Internal Promotion Rate, Vacancy Rate, Performance Rating Average, Leave Utilization Rate, Early Attrition Rate, Recruitment Source Effectiveness
