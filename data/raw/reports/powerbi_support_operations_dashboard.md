# Power BI Dashboard: Support Operations Dashboard
**Domain:** Customer
**Pages:** Ticket Volume, SLA, Agent Performance
**Key Measure:** SLA Compliance
**DAX:** SLA Compliance = DIVIDE([Tickets Within SLA], [Total Tickets])
**Filters:** Date range slicer, Region slicer
**Scheduled Refresh:** Every 3 hours
**KPIs Included:** Customer Retention Rate, Customer Acquisition Cost, Net Promoter Score, Customer Effort Score, Average Resolution Time, Ticket Backlog, Escalation Rate, Active Customers, Reactivated Customers, Customer Concentration Ratio, Complaint Rate, SLA Compliance Rate, Cart Abandonment Rate, Campaign Conversion Rate, Cost per Lead
