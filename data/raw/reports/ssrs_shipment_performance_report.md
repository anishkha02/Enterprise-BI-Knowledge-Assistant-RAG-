# SSRS Report: Shipment Performance Report
**Domain:** Operations
**Purpose:** Paginated Operations report for operational review.
**Parameters:** Carrier, Date Range
**Datasets:** dim_product, fact_gl, fact_inventory, fact_orders, fact_shipments
**Refresh Schedule:** Daily at 8:00 AM
**KPIs Included:** Days Inventory Outstanding, Order Fulfillment Rate, Perfect Order Rate, Shipping Cost per Order, Backorder Rate, Carrying Cost of Inventory, Supplier Lead Time, Defect Rate, Production Throughput, Machine Downtime, Scrap Rate, Return to Vendor Rate, Dock to Stock Time, Obsolete Inventory Rate, Damaged Shipment Rate
