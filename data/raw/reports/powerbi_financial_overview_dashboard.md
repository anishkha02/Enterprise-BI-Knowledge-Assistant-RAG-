# Power BI Dashboard: Financial Overview Dashboard
**Domain:** Finance
**Pages:** Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow
**Key Measure:** Gross Margin
**DAX:** Gross Margin = DIVIDE([Gross Profit], [Revenue])
**Filters:** Date range slicer, Region slicer
**Scheduled Refresh:** Daily at 5:00 AM
**KPIs Included:** Gross Profit, Net Profit, Operating Profit, EBITDA, Operating Expense Ratio, Budget Variance, Return on Assets, Return on Investment, Quick Ratio, Days Sales Outstanding, Cash Conversion Cycle, Free Cash Flow, Accounts Receivable Turnover, Interest Coverage Ratio, Capital Expenditure Ratio
