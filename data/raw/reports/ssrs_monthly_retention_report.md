# SSRS Report: Monthly Retention Report
**Domain:** Customer
**Purpose:** Paginated Customer report for operational review.
**Parameters:** Cohort Month, Segment
**Datasets:** dim_customer, fact_gl, fact_marketing, fact_orders, fact_surveys, fact_tickets
**Refresh Schedule:** Monthly on the 2nd
**KPIs Included:** Customer Churn Rate, Customer Lifetime Value, CLV to CAC Ratio, Customer Satisfaction Score, First Response Time, First Contact Resolution Rate, Ticket Volume, Reopen Rate, New Customers, Average Revenue per Customer, Repeat Customer Rate, Survey Response Rate, Customer Health Score, Email Open Rate, Marketing ROI
